The game

One click.
Total chaos.

You're in the turret at the centre of the arena. It fires on its own — you click where you want it to face and it swings round to cover that gate. Easy to pick up. Near-impossible to hold.

  1. Aim, don't shoot. Eight gates, aliens from every side. Pick the gate that's about to break.
  2. Build the combo. Kill streaks climb all the way to x8 — one hit on the turret and it's gone.
  3. Upgrade between runs. Every kill drops cash: firing speed, damage, multi-shot, rotation, health and more.

Save the scientists

Break glass in
case of aliens.

Panicking scientists sprint for your turret — and the aliens grab them. Clear a path and get them home for an emergency power-up. Hold one of each. Spend them wisely.

Sometimes a rescued scientist brings a heart instead — full health, right when you need it.

Know your invader

Seven ways
to be overrun.

Every ten waves is a new area with its own colours, its own music and its own new alien — which then never goes away.

WAVES 1–10

The Grunt

A Piece of Cake

Squat, blobby and big-headed. One on its own is nothing. They never come on their own.

WAVES 11–20

The Heavy

Chunky Monkey

Hulking knuckle-walkers that freeze, flash and swell up as they close in. Keep firing.

WAVES 21–30

The Runner

Speed is of the Essence

Spindly four-legged skitterers. Fragile, frantic, and they punish a moment's hesitation.

WAVES 31–40

The Splitter

A Splitting Headache

Kill it and it bursts into a handful of fast little Splitlings — who come straight back.

WAVES 41–50

The Bomber

Bombtastic

Carries a bomb to your turret and plants it. Shoot it first and the blast takes its friends with it.

WAVES 51–60

Shield-bearer

Shields Up

Marches in behind an energy shield that soaks your bullets — and covers everyone behind it.

WAVES 61–70

The Tunneller

Digging their own Grave

Skips the queue. Digs in, ripples under your wall, and bursts up inside the arena.

…and all of them at once.

    the Darkside.
